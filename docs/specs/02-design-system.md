# 02 Design system

Use the UI UX Pro Max skill.

## Goal
Portfolio landing page for a Strategic Marketing student (Hazel). Audience:
recruiters and potential clients/agencies. Personal and creative, but her case
studies must be easy to scan.

## Sections (in order)
1. Hero: name, one-line positioning, CTA ("See my work" / "Contact")
2. Featured projects / case studies (cards: image, title, tags, short result)
3. About + experience (short CV timeline)
4. Skills
5. Contact + social links

## Visual direction: editorial base, playful accents
Two reference images are attached in chat. Extract principles, do NOT clone.
- Playful reference: take ONLY the accents: thick outlines (~3px), hard
  offset shadows (no blur, ~4px 4px), sticker-like shapes, bold accent color.
  Sparingly: hero, buttons, tags, 1-2 decorative shapes max.
- Editorial reference: take the STRUCTURE: generous whitespace, strong grid,
  thin dividers, high-contrast type, image-led project cards.
- Overall mood: soft pastel sky, fluffy clouds, pink and sky-blue with gold
  accents. Do NOT use any existing character, mascot, or brand imagery.
- The Sky Dial (spec 03) is the signature element; keep other decoration calm.

## Starting tokens (refine with the skill)
- Background: warm off-white #FAF6F0; Ink: #1E1E1E
- Accents: pink #F28FD0, yellow #F5CE4A, periwinkle #8E9BE6, plus a pastel
  sky-blue and a soft gold. One accent per section.
- Headings: heavy geometric grotesk; body: clean neutral sans (General Sans
  or similar). Propose 2 pairings.
- Radius 12-16px on cards, pill shape on tags/buttons.

## Constraints
Mobile-first, WCAG AA contrast, prefers-reduced-motion respected.

## PROCESS
1. Propose the design system only: palette, type pairing, spacing scale,
   button/card/tag styles. Show it and STOP for approval.
2. Then restyle the hero only. STOP.
3. Then the remaining sections one at a time.
